## Build this

1. **Page:** title "Load a sector", subtitle "Build a company roster, review the names, then start collection." Under it, a stepper: 1 Roster, 2 Review names, 3 Choose cycles, 4 Collect. Circles 36px joined by 1px `var(--line)` rules; active = filled `--acu-plum` with a white numeral; done = filled `--acu-plum` with a white check; upcoming = a 1px outline. Labels 16px, the active one weight 600. Step 4 is the Collection page, which Start collection already opens.
2. **Two columns:** a wide left card (about two thirds) and the narrow "Collection setup" card (one third). The right card always summarises and holds the step's one gold button.
3. **Step 1, Roster (B20 and L1).** A `.segmented` "Type names | Upload a spreadsheet" at the top of the left card.
   - Type names (B20): the "Sector" field (`#sector`); an editable table with the columns "Company name" and "Other names searched" (chips: Enter or a comma adds one, × removes) and a remove icon per row; a last row "Add a company..."; buttons "+ Add company" (`#add-company`) and "Paste from spreadsheet" (new: reads tab- or comma-separated text from the clipboard into rows, first column the name, the rest aliases); a dashed drop zone "Drop a roster file (.xlsx or .csv)" that feeds the upload path; "Draft stays in this browser." (`#draft-status`). The table replaces the stacked company blocks; `draftData()` must still return exactly `{sector, companies:[{name, aliases}]}`, and a saved draft must still restore.
   - Upload (L1): the uploaded-file row (file icon, name, "31 rows · 10 companies · 1 sector", text links "Replace" and "Remove"); a column check of four chips, "canonical_name -> Company name", "alias -> Other names", "sector -> Sector", "exclusion -> Excluded names", each with a teal check (an amber cross and the server's message when a required column is missing); a preview table (Company name | Other names | Sector | Excluded) of the first six companies and "and N more companies"; an amber note for problems the review will raise ("... You can settle it in step 2."); at the foot "CSV, TSV or Excel · up to 5 MB · up to 10,000 rows" and "Download the upload template" (`/template.csv`).
   - Right card: "Sector: <name>", "<n> companies · <m> names to search", `--muted` "Nothing is collected until you press Start collection.", and gold "Review names ->" (the `#review-roster` action).
4. **Step 2, Review names (L2).** The left card "Names that will be searched" with the count line (`#roster-counts`: "10 companies · 31 search names · 1 sector"). At its top, an amber-outlined box "<k> names need a decision" with one row per problem from `readiness`:
   - `cross_company_variants`: "<NAME> is listed under <A> and <B>", with buttons "Keep for <A>", "Keep for <B>" and "Remove".
   - `duplicate_companies`: "<NAME> appears twice", with "Merge" and "Remove".
   - `parse_errors`: the message, with "Fix in step 1".
   Each button edits the draft and re-runs `/api/roster`. This replaces the raw-JSON line at `app.js:195`.
   Below, the roster grouped by sector (`#roster-list`): the company name in bold, its search names as uppercase chips, exclusions as muted chips "Exclude: <NAME>", and an amber dot on any name involved in an open decision.
   Right card: "<sector> · <n> companies · <m> names", `--muted` "Settle <k> names to continue", secondary "Back", and gold "Choose cycles ->", disabled until every group reports `ready`.
5. **Step 3, Choose cycles (L3).** The left card "Election cycles": three checkbox cards side by side (`name=cycle`, values 2022, 2024 and 2026, labelled "2021-22", "2023-24", "2025-26"), each showing this sector's saved coverage from the coverage data: `current` -> a teal check and "Saved: verified through <date>"; `stale` -> amber "Saved: verified through <date> · stale"; `unverifiable` with evidence -> a grey hatched mark and "Saved: coverage unverifiable"; no saved evidence -> a dashed circle and "Not collected yet". Then a row "FEC API keys" with a teal dot and "16 enabled · 4 disabled" (from the key status) and a text link "Add or replace keys" that reveals the existing `#api-key` field and "Save keys securely" (`#save-keys`). Then "What happens next", three numbered lines: "Searches <m> names across <c> cycles", "Saves checkpoints as it runs; closing the browser does not stop collection", "Progress appears in Collection". Right card: the summary, secondary "Back", and gold "Start collection" (`#start`, with the same payload and behaviour).
6. **Saved FEC coverage (L4)**, a full-width card under the flow: title "Saved FEC coverage", subtitle "What is already on this computer, by sector and cycle", and "How coverage is assessed" (today's text, in a popover). A matrix with one row per sector exactly as the data names it (do not merge "Credit Unions" and "Credit Union") and one column per cycle. Each cell is one chip: `current` -> teal "Verified · to <verified_data_through>"; `stale` -> amber outline "Stale · to <date>"; `unverifiable` with evidence -> grey with fine diagonal hatching, "Unverifiable"; no evidence -> dashed "Not collected". One legend line under the matrix. Clicking a cell outlines it in `--acu-purple` and fills a detail panel at the right of the same card: "<Sector> · <cycle>", Verified data-through, Status, Latest observed transaction, Roster, the reason sentence, and a secondary "Coverage evidence" button that shows the per-company evidence list. Keep `#loading-coverage` as the container id.
