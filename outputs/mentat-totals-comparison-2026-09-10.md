## Evidence and uncertainty

The diagnostic applies the current receipt-role and memo rules to the pinned completed-report sources, using private recovery copies where metadata was obtained. Prior recipient, party and employee-tier classifications are held fixed to isolate the corrections. Previously excluded memo rows re-enter only if the current eligibility predicate allows them. Other historical exclusion categories remain excluded. These are reported receipt-stage totals under the existing treatment of intermediaries and amendments, not a reconstruction of unique end-to-end dollars.

The initial crypto diagnostic showed why missing evidence cannot be treated as permission to add everything: five large memo rows would have added $116,999,995. FEC API evidence and two exact transactions in the original [Fairshake filing 1940775](https://docquery.fec.gov/dcdev/posted/1940775.fec) identify those rows as other-receipt/disclosure entries. They remain excluded. The $24,999,995 in-kind contribution and $23 million in-kind contribution remain included; the separate $25 million sale proceeds do not count as another contribution.

Metadata was recovered for 25 additional source IDs in this comparison: 23 through the FEC API and two from the original filing. Financial identity was checked before updating metadata in private copies. Original databases and workbooks were not changed.
