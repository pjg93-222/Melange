# Primacy → Efficiency → Substance → Volume

Corrected isolated candidate. Production rankings and the earlier three-factor run are unchanged.

Frozen evidence cutoff: 2026-09-06. Baseline: **reproduced**.

Ranking follows the stated priorities in order, with no scalar blend. Efficiency is progressive five-stage credit normalized within each historical chamber. Substance is the average impact class of beneficial engagements. Volume counts beneficial engagement after role and joining-time adjustments, without impact weighting. Missing higher-priority evidence prevents lower criteria from improving that member's position.

Institutional position has no tier-scoring field or multiplier. Real legislative milestones still count. Existing evidence, conduct, override and fixed-seat rules are applied after the analytical ordering.

**Substance limitation:** selected bills currently have provisional weight 5. Equal average substance is a tie, not a finding that every bill is equally important. The publication evidence does not yet support significance upgrades. The 180-day cosponsor half-life is the existing illustrative setting, not a validated optimum.

## Interpretation

The priority order is strict: efficiency, substance and volume only resolve ties in preceding criteria. A stronger lower-priority score cannot compensate for lower primacy. Changes in those tie-breakers need not change the highest tiers.

118th and 119th Congress are separate retrospective comparisons using the current published primacy board. They are not out-of-sample validation or estimates of individual causal impact. Unverified letter dates remain coverage records, with no invented legislative stages.
