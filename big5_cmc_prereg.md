# Big-5 Cross-Method Convergence (CMC) — Pre-Registration

**Locked: 2026-07-08, BEFORE the correlations below are computed. Committed to lock the bar.**

## Motivation
The independent literature review (deep-research, 2026-07-08; 22 primary sources, adversarially
verified) graded the Pink instrument **A- / high-A within the elite-speech text-personality class**.
Its one standing reviewer objection: the convergent validity of r=.42-.52 is measured against an
elite dataset that is itself TEXT-derived (RKH LIWC-style scores) — i.e. **shared-method** (text vs
text). A skeptical reviewer discounts shared-method convergence relative to convergence against an
INDEPENDENT, non-text method. This test attacks that objection directly.

## Design
Pink (LLM item-read of speech = a TEXT-JUDGE method) is correlated against the behavioral
dispositions (roll-call participation + cosponsorship structure = a BEHAVIORAL method Pink was never
fit to — instrument independence intact). Same-trait diagonal = cross-method convergent validity.
Off-diagonal = discriminant. This is a Campbell-Fiske cross-method MTMM with a genuinely independent
second method.

Trait map (Pink `raw_z[t]` vs behavioral z): **A←cooperation_z, C←diligence_z, E←visibility_z,
O←breadth_z.** Pearson, complete cases, n≈509. ES excluded (no behavioral criterion exists — the
same honest gap documented for CTCM2). Blue is fit to these behavioral targets and is therefore NOT
a valid comparator here; only Pink (independent of them) is tested.

## Blind status (honesty ledger)
- **A, E, O: BLIND** — Pink's traits have never been correlated against the behavioral dispositions.
  The primary convergent bar rests on these three.
- **C: NOT blind** — Pink-C already has prior positive behavioral-criterion evidence (retest vs
  votes_cast +.155, vs follow-through +.112, @18ce3b3). C is reported as a CONFIRMATION, not a
  discovery; it is included in the discriminant matrix but excluded from the primary convergent bar.

## Bars (ONE look, locked)
1. **CONVERGENT (primary):** mean same-trait diagonal r over {A, E, O} **≥ +0.12**, AND each of
   A, E, O individually **≥ +0.05** (right-signed and non-trivial). Rationale for the level: the
   text→self-report ceiling is ~.38 (Park/Schwartz); behavior is a noisier criterion than
   self-report so cross-method text→behavior sits below that; the established Pink-C behavioral
   criterion (~+.15) anchors the plausible range. +0.12 is mid-range: real signal required, not a
   gimme.
2. **DISCRIMINANT (MTMM direction):** mean same-trait diagonal over {A, C, E, O} **>** mean
   |off-diagonal| (convergent exceeds heterotrait — the Campbell-Fiske direction). This is where a
   halo-laden Pink-A would fail; the risk is real.

## Outcomes
- **PASS (both bars):** the shared-method objection is ANSWERED for A/E/O — Pink converges with an
  independent behavioral method, not only with other text. Documented in big5_methods.md as a
  defensibility upgrade; the grade firms from A- toward A. **A claimed pass is adversarially
  re-derived (independent recomputation) before it is recorded — the P6 lesson, now doctrine.**
- **MISS (either bar):** Pink's convergence is partly text-method-bound for the missing trait(s);
  documented honestly, grade stays A- with the shared-method caveat standing. No number moves.
