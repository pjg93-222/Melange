## Remaining system-audit priorities

1. Reconcile statement admission and coverage before interpreting low media counts as weak advocacy. Distinguish absent evidence, neutral evidence and off-topic evidence explicitly; the committed historical corpus does not automatically admit new items.
2. Review bill-specific vote directions and bind the current configuration to the artifact receipt. The prior audit's known-direction-only comparison covers just one measure and cannot substitute for a completed vote model.
3. Demonstrate that eligibility and conduct constraints hold together for all possible seating cases. The prior audit identified sequential reseating and insufficient-qualifier fallback risks; current exact reproduction is not proof against those latent cases.
4. Ask whether legislative depth is confidence alone or an additional form of merit. The current grade still rewards activity and breadth, even after beneficiary weighting. Establish that meaning before adjusting another coefficient.
5. Separate explicit national ACU champion wording, electoral backing, policy credit and incidental association coverage. Use the external labels for comparison and disagreement review, rather than treating them as automatic truth or score inputs.
6. Resolve the remaining arithmetic contracts: the signed tensor multiplier treats equal positive and negative cells asymmetrically; amendment amounts differ between factor and grade; letters do not satisfy the direct-legislation floor; and that floor counts relevant credit without requiring positive direction. Each issue needs an explicit common unit or evidence definition before another scoring change.
7. Guard latent input cases: a negative factor reverses the benefit of a higher grade under the current product, and missing tenure receives a favorable .5-year denominator. Neither case occurs in the frozen 533-member set, so exact parity cannot exercise them. Also distinguish a positive relative percentile from observed positive advocacy in an area with no evidence.

These priorities carry forward findings from the frozen system audit. They are outstanding work, not defects claimed repaired by this promotion.
