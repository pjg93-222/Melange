## Shipped behavior

- CU eligibility and conduct constraints combine by the strictest minimum permitted slot before a single seating pass. A compact layout that cannot satisfy them raises an explicit error. This also closes inconsistent rank/tier behavior in the shadow realized-results path.
- Numeric evidence is validated both at artifact loading and direct scoring/seating APIs. Booleans, nonfinite values, invalid grade percentiles and negative quantities in nonnegative domains cannot become valid scores or restrictions. Genuine signed factors remain valid in factor-only and legacy-product modes.
- The CU per-area product refuses negative factors until a signed-domain rule is specified. It does not clip opposition or silently reverse the benefit of a higher grade.
- Missing or malformed service dates stop grade generation. A documented first day and known short service retain the .5-year denominator floor. Overlapping terms remain counted once.
- An explicit zero conduct leverage stays zero; only an absent leverage field defaults to one. Active conduct/delivery arithmetic refuses overflow and malformed inputs.
- Non-CU evidence priority is now implemented explicitly in the industry producer. Its existing Tier 1 qualification check remains mandatory by default; lower tiers retain the previous soft preference. This preserves the existing 23 bank, 40 crypto and 6 fintech qualifiers without weakening CU eligibility.

Existing explicit-placement behavior remains for the older role-mass gate: pins can exempt that gate, but conduct applies. The new affirmative Tier 1 gate described below has no pin exemption. This repair does not invent immutable pin positions or a vacant-seat schema. Existing supported method and board switches remain available; invalid numeric evidence and infeasible joint constraints remain errors.

This is a two-cycle oscillation, not a change to the scoring equation.
