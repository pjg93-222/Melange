# ACU cross-reference follow-up — September 20, 2026

These findings add external context to the grading sheet. They do not change the approved scoring inputs, classifications or ranks. The main comparison covers 2024–2026; historical CUNA evidence is dated separately.

## What the external benchmark measures

ACU's September 9, 2026 announcement describes an electoral goal of over 340 CU champions and support already provided to 303 individual candidates. It also identifies targeted general-election investments, including Ciscomani. [ACU announcement](https://www.americascreditunions.org/news-media/news/americas-credit-unions-and-its-pac-invests-10-million-2026-midterm-election).

That broad electoral coalition is not the same construct or roster as this board's 13 Tier 1 seats. This is an inference about benchmark scope, not a disagreement with the reported support. Report individual praise, electoral backing, dated policy acts and incidental coverage separately; do not treat any one as an automatic Tier 1 label. The [official elections hub](https://www.americascreditunions.org/advocacy/pac/elections-hub/election) has a state-based interactive list. After text-only retrieval missed its entries, browser inspection on September 20 verified ten state tables: California, Oregon, Nevada, New York, Minnesota, Missouri, Texas, Idaho, Tennessee and Maine. This is a targeted follow-up, not a complete national PAC-roster reconciliation.

Search-indexed release text was available in this pass; direct page opens returned cache misses. The two ACU pages were also opened and read directly.

Further searches did not establish qualifying 2024–2026 national ACU individual champion wording for Costa, Bonamici, Carbajal, Amodei, Brownley, Finstad, Golden, Wagner, Ogles, Morelle, Cuellar or Fulcher. This is a bounded search result, not a finding that they lack CU support; the eight current PAC findings above resolve electoral backing while leaving the more specific champion wording unverified.

The distinction is consequential for the full system audit: collecting an article that mentions a member, or reporting that advocates contacted that member, must not manufacture a supportive action attributable to the member. Historical praise can guide review, but should not silently substitute for current evidence.
