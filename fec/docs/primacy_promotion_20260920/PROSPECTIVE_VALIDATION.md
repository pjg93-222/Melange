# Proposed validation contract

This is a proposal for the next methodology discussion, not a ratified scoring change. Correct arithmetic, a defensible interpretation and useful prediction are different claims. Passing one does not establish the others.

1. **Specify the quantity first.** For every component, declare the observed universe, date window, event unit, denominator, direction, relevance, role weights and missing-data treatment. The factor uses a role-weighted classified portfolio with amendment half-mass. The grade uses annualized action plus media and chamber percentiles. The current press statistic uses classified occurrence intensity, retaining crossposts and third-party material. A “share of CU support” needs an explicit denominator.

2. **Test invariants before placements.** Require monotonicity at fixed evidence mass, valid bounds/domains, jointly binding restrictions, missing-versus-zero distinctions, the selected opposition rule and the declared duplicate-handling behavior. Correct unit conversions should preserve the stated estimand. Crossposts can increase communication exposure without becoming independent commitments.

3. **Prespecify a few alternatives and inspect them blind.** Freeze source inputs. Compare one substantive rule at a time, initially using anonymized portfolios and paired counterexamples. Write the evaluation criterion before revealing member names, parties or ACU recognition. The accepted current board is the defect-repair baseline, not the answer key for evaluating a new method.

4. **Publish sensitivity, not a tuned point.** Examine an intelligible range for each disputed coefficient or threshold. Report stable placements, unstable boundaries, tier crossings, eligibility changes and chamber composition for the entire roster. These are scenario ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. Explain movements through actual contribution/rule changes; ordinal rank differences are not additive causal attribution.

5. **Respect dependence and coverage.** Define substantive message/event clusters and measure families before resampling or holding out evidence. Bills, amendments, reintroductions and associated votes can be related; crossposts are not independent observations merely because URLs differ. Any bootstrap would estimate uncertainty conditional on the collected corpus and clustering assumptions. It cannot recover unobserved activity or establish representative coverage. The existing shrinkage coefficient is not a probability that a member is a champion.

6. **Use a later, independently adjudicated assessment.** Freeze the rule and information cutoff, then grade new CU-relevant acts using a prespecified rubric with reviewers blind to rankings and champion labels. Keep a substantive event/measure family from appearing on both sides through another channel. Evaluate alignment conditional on an observed, relevant opportunity; report action volume and leadership separately. No observed action is not automatically opposition. Prediction supplements the normative definition of advocacy rather than proving it.

7. **Treat the ACU comparison as partially dependent.** `primacy_factors.act_weight` already uses declared `acu_position` and ACU relevance rules in applicable pathways, subject to operative-text tensor precedence. ACU recognition may reflect the same policy information. Agreement is therefore corroboration, not wholly independent validation. Retain the distinction between explicit champion language, electoral support, policy credit and incidental coverage. Never choose coefficients or adjudicate held-out acts to improve ACU agreement. An ablation excluding ACU-derived inputs could measure that dependence as a separate sensitivity exercise; it is not an adopted replacement board.

For each proposed candidate, retain its source hashes, invariant results, all member movements, disagreement cases and measurement limitations alongside favorable benchmark results. A desirable ranking produced by an unexplained equation is not a sufficient result.
