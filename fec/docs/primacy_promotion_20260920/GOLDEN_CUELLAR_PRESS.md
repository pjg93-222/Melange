This review reads the frozen statement text behind the adopted ledger. The media component includes social posts and official releases. It is not a tally of favorable news coverage. No classification, weight or placement is changed here.

The three general CFPB occurrences still contribute to the denominator, but their OFF_TOPIC class sets their numerator contribution to zero. The CU endorsement contributes about .6667; the shared overdraft opposition subtracts .00281. Dividing by all admitted intensity mass (about 2.07229) yields approximately **+.32035**. This is an intensity- and class-weighted signed ratio, not the percentage of statements endorsing credit unions.

His improvement reflects ceasing to treat broad CFPB defense as direct CU opposition in this diagnostic. It does not reflect discovery of numerous new CU endorsements. The low .00562 intensity assigned to the official overdraft release, versus .66667 for the social endorsement, also matters materially. Those inherited cross-channel intensity units remain worth examining; this review does not silently equalize them.

He also sits exactly at the retained five-occurrence media eligibility threshold. The five occurrences represent four messages because the February 12 statement is crossposted. If eligibility counted these messages once while retaining the same threshold, this record would fall below it. This is a count-level sensitivity observation, not a new calculated rank or a proposal to silently reverse the adopted crosspost policy. Independent-message depth and occurrence-weighted support share should be distinguished when reviewing confidence and eligibility.

## Interpretation and external praise

Cuellar's admitted record contains repeated explicit CU-support commitments. Golden's contains one clear endorsement of the cooperative ownership model alongside regulatory disagreements. Neither record, by itself, establishes Tier 1. Both currently remain at the placements Paul accepted.

Separately, a March 2020 CUNA release explicitly called Cuellar a longtime CU champion; Golden received CUNA electoral advertising support in October 2020. These are historical external judgments, not current scoring inputs. See [ACU follow-up](ACU_FOLLOWUP.md) for dated links and the distinction between champion wording and electoral backing.
