## Introduced-text verification

Record URL, version, retrieval UTC, raw SHA-256 and errors. Use the legislative body only, excluding metadata and introductory sponsor lists. Preserve substantive numbers and dates in normalized body tokens. Text first observed now is not represented as historically observed now; the same event-time reconstruction limitation applies.

Compute exact normalized-body equality, five-token shingle Dice similarity and body-length ratio. The prespecified close-text rule is Dice >= 0.90 and length ratio >= 0.80, with at least 40 tokens in both bodies. This verifies textual resemblance, not legal equivalence. Missing text is missing verification, not a dissimilar bill. Do not search for alternative predecessors using outcomes.
