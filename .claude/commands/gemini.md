# /gemini — route a perception task through the Gemini lane

Load the `gemini-perception` skill and follow it exactly.

Usage: `/gemini <task involving images, video, audio, screenshots, or scans>`

- Claude writes the perception contract and adjudicates the report.
- Gemini captures and comprehends; it decides nothing and writes no code.
- Computation, splicing, and marking route onward to the Codex executor
  lane under its full contract.
- If the Antigravity CLI is absent or unauthenticated, say so loudly and
  stop; never substitute another model for the perception step without
  Paul's instruction.
