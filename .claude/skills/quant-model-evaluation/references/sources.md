## How the entries were verified

- **DOIs (434).** Each was fetched on 2026-09-30 from the CrossRef REST API public pool (`/works/<doi>`, sequential, no contact details sent; the two added by the final sweep, Treloar et al. 2026 and the 10.1080 record of Ansolabehere et al. 2002, the same way); the ten DataCite DOIs (arXiv, Zenodo, FORCE11 and a university repository) were resolved through OpenAlex singleton records. First author, year (print or online, ±1), title (main title at least) and venue were compared with how the skill cites the work, and volume, issue and first page with the 425 module entries that state them.
- **Currency.** CrossRef `updated-by` notices and OpenAlex `is_retracted` were read for every DOI on 2026-09-30. No retraction, withdrawal or expression of concern was indexed that day. "flag C: … notice listed" means a correction, erratum or addendum notice is indexed (10 entries): read it before quoting details. Absence of a notice means none was indexed that day, not that the work is sound.
- **Works without a DOI** were matched to an OpenAlex record (ID given), to CrossRef-indexed book reviews, or to the issuer's page. **Legal and official sources** were read on the issuer's site on the date shown; the register rows not re-read here keep the source research's date.
- **Not graded here:** whether a source supports the sentence that cites it (axis S of `citation.md` §2.1). Grade that per use.

**Status tags.** `VERIFIED@2026-09-30`: identity matched on that date. `VERIFIED@2026-09-29`: confirmed by the source research on that date and not re-read. `CORRECTED`: the work is verified, but a citation of it in this skill needs the fix named in the note (none at present: the four script citations the review flagged have been fixed, and their notes say how). `UNVERIFIED`: not confirmed; do not rely on it. Entries: 446 works plus the legal, service, unverified and pointer lists below. Script line numbers were re-checked by the final sweep on 2026-09-30.
