    Explore the recorded legs. Spending from a pooled PAC is committee activity; it cannot be assigned to a particular donor without further evidence.

    ESTIMATION METHOD

    Proportional share of available cash

    At each payment, assign the same fraction as the company's share of the PAC's available cash. Carry that fraction through PAC transfers; leave retained cash and operating expenses in their own categories.

    Estimated company-funded spending = payment × company cash share before payment

    Example only: a 25% cash share × $10 million of candidate spending gives a $2.5 million estimate. This is an accounting assumption, not proof of donor intent or influence.
