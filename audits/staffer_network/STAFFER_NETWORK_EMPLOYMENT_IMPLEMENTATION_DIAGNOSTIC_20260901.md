## Official covered-position scope: date-version correction

The phrase “official LDA two-year covered-position limitation” must be applied
with a filing-vintage qualifier. Section 4 of the original 1995 LDA required a
position disclosure when the employee had served as a covered executive- or
legislative-branch official in the **two years** before first acting as a
lobbyist for that client. Current House LDA Guidance, reflecting the later
Honest Leadership and Open Government Act expansion, uses a **20-year**
lookback. The original two-year rule is therefore a historical rule, not the
current universal rule.

Both versions impose the same modeling limitation: the field is a selective,
client-relative lookback for covered government service. It is not a complete
career history; it does not report the lobbyist's current private-sector role;
and it does not require episode start/end dates. A filing date is an observation
date, never an employment or departure date. Blank later filings do not negate
an earlier disclosed covered position.

Official authorities consulted:

- House LDA Guidance: <https://lobbyingdisclosure.house.gov/ldaguidance.pdf>
- Original LDA text, section 4 registration requirements:
  <https://lobbyingdisclosure.house.gov/lda.html>

## Employment implementation framework

### Evidence and episode planes

Retain raw observations separately from adjudication and derived episodes. An
observation binds source system/person, source locator and digest, retrieval and
source-as-of times, literal person/employer/title/date values, normalized
uncertain bounds and precision, currentness semantics, optional member/office
or organization identifiers, and provenance IDs.

Normalize every episode to uncertain half-open day intervals. Preserve literal
precision; never turn an unknown month or day into a known one. A current/open
interval is right-censored at the source/build as-of boundary. Unknown bounds
remain structural-only and receive no temporal weight. Union compatible
intervals by person and accepted container before computing overlap; repeated
payroll or filing support cannot multiply time or person contributions.

### Binding employment-completeness gate

`COMPLETE` requires, at minimum, a nonempty person ID; valid employer/office and
title for every employment episode; valid source-supported start and end bounds
or explicit currentness; an exhaustive employment attestation bound by digest
to the exact merged episode set; no unresolved typed or legacy employment gaps;
and adjudication `PASS`. Malformed dates, non-HTTP(S) external locators,
unresolved source/container evidence, stale episode digests, or any collected
gap fail closed.

Education has its own diagnostics and attestation surface but cannot appear in
employment `failure_codes`. The current pinned verification records **48
focused tests and 222 staffer-network tests plus 73 subtests, all passing with
zero failures**. Those tests and the five resolving Opus constituent reviews

## Current graph-math rulings

- **Direct support:** canonical structural facts have weight 1; duplicate
  endpoint/layer evidence collapses by maximum. The tested per-source noisy-OR
  support combiner is not wired into production and must not be described as a
  live weight or identity probability.
- **Traversal:** each node allocates equal mass to each active layer, then
  proportionally within the layer. Bidirectional traversal is descriptive;
  source-fact direction remains intact.
- **Connectedness:** primary `connectedness_pagerank` uses alpha 0.85 with 0.70
  and 0.95 sensitivity, entity-kind-balanced teleport, and excludes all
  identity candidates. Isolates retain teleport-only mass and are excluded from
  rankings.
- **Eigenvector diagnostic:** solve `(P + P.T)/2` independently by connected
  component, L1-normalize within component, emit nonconverged components as
  null, and never globally rank values across components.
- **Bridging:** normalized Simpson diversity across neighbor entity kinds is
  reported separately from strength/PageRank and is not directly comparable
  across entity kinds.
- **Co-tenure:** union uncertain half-open episode cores/envelopes before
  intersection. Guaranteed overlap `G` supplies the primary lower weight
  `1-exp(-G/365.2425)`; possible-only overlap has primary weight zero. Keep the
  corresponding possible-overlap upper sensitivity.
- **Revolving door:** require an adjudicated person, adjudicated
  employer/container, and a guaranteed Hill-before-industry order. Primary
  member–organization weight is the unique-person count. Gap decay is only a
  sensitivity interval, with the declared 730.485-day half-life. Registrant
  employment and client-activity participation remain distinct predicates.
  If any necessary ordering bound is unknown, emit one deterministic
  `INTERVAL_BLOCKED` review path with null gap bounds, zero primary/possible
  weight, no revolving edge, and an explicitly conserved receipt count; never
  evaluate an empty candidate minimum or demote unknown order to wrong-order.
- **Composite:** no composite influence score. Structural connectedness cannot
  be labeled influence, access, friendship, ideology, or causation.

Temporal graph scores remain closed while the employment evidence stays on the
review-candidate plane.
