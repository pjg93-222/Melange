What changes, against the five aims

  • Ease of use. Graph: one filter row, one primary action, everything else behind ⋯. Chart Studio: two chrome rows instead of five; the chart is visible without scrolling on a 1440×900 screen.
  • Conciseness. Graph loses the repeated scope sentence, the global "Rows per chart" input and the export target/format selects (48 to about 30 visible controls). Chart Studio loses three duplicate entry points (Show Me toggle + section + Chart variant select become one), "Add to briefing" appears once, Save/Open view move under Views, Classic builder moves under Export (87 to about 55).
  • Symmetry. Equal side panels 280 | flex | 280 with the same padding and section labels; Graph panels share one header layout (title left, Top N and View all right) and one footer line; KPI tiles are equal thirds.
  • De-cluttering. Helper sentences move into ⓘ tooltips and panel footers; caps-lock summaries become chips; the stale-build banner keeps its place (system, untouched).
  • Depth of visualization. Share-of-scope beside every bar, party colour on candidates, sparklines per company across cycles, a stacked level bar, hover detail, and in Chart Studio a Split by shelf that renders small multiples. Every figure keeps its window and denominator on screen.

Numbers are the real Banking figures from the console on 2026-09-17; sparkline and per-cycle splits are illustrative shapes, the real ones come from the workbook.
