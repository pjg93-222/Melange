WHY THE EARLIER RUNS FAILED AT STEP 5, AND WHAT WAS ACTUALLY BROKEN
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 5 (import six.csv ~6 MB) timed out twice at 60 s. That was (a) HOST LOAD:
on a quiet host the same step passes in 2.4 s. The identical 6 MB ingest measured
between 0.82 s and 34.66 s on this machine depending on what else was running.
Orphaned consoles from the aborted runs were still resident (one on port 8943 with
a temp data root) and free memory was 1.6 GB of 31.5 GB.

Getting past step 5 then exposed two REAL defects, both mine, now fixed:

1. SERVER, evidence_csv.py Store.read_record - reported a live import as gone.
   Polling ?job= during the 40 MB ingest returned 'This CSV import is no longer on
   disk (csv-b0de...)' while the directory AND import.json were both present on
   disk (proved by listing the store at the moment of the error). read_record
   caught OSError and returned None, and a transient Windows sharing violation on
   import.json - which the ingest rewrites for progress every 1000 rows while the
   page polls it twice a second - was being reported as a permanent 'gone'.
   Fix: read_record retries a losing read briefly (5 attempts, 0.02-0.08 s apart);
   a genuinely absent file (FileNotFoundError) still answers immediately.

2. CLIENT, evidence-csv.js followJob - gave up after a single failed poll.
   Even with the server fixed, one transient blip stranded the page on an error
   while the import went on finishing invisibly behind it.
   Fix: keep the last good state and retry; stop after pollFailureLimit = 5
   consecutive failures, leaving the message on screen.

3. TEST FIXTURE, test_evidence_csv_browser.cjs - step 6 was unpassable as written.
   forty.csv used ~77-byte rows, so 40 MB came to 516,377 rows and the server
   correctly refused the file with 'Choose a CSV containing no more than 500,000
   rows.' MAX_ROWS binds alongside the 50 MB ceiling. The fixture now uses rows of
   a realistic Schedule A width (~185 B): 6 MB -> 32,515 rows, 40 MB -> 212,985
   rows. The test's assertions are unchanged.
