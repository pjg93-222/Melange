## What a full "yes" does

- Unblocks every industry's rebuild at once (all eight refusal sets go to zero except R2/R17 rows, which need a hand look).
- Changes published Total Giving only through R7 (a), R8, R10, R11 and R13 (a): about $3.3M in, almost all of it inaugural donations (R7) and party special-account gifts (R11). Everything else is money the books already keep out.
- Prevents a $132.5M Crypto double count (R3) and a $35M non-contribution (R4) from ever entering a Crypto rebuild.

## Second blocker, independent of the rulings

The live exporter cannot load any of the eight databases today: `report.R:2032` and `:2051` select `contributor_street_1`/`contributor_street_2` unconditionally (commit `d78cf0b8f`), and none of the 16 tables has those columns. The patch `gate_census/report_R_street_tolerant.patch` routes the two street columns through the same tolerant `NULL AS <field>` projection already used for absent receipt metadata (lifted into one `optional_projection()` helper). Proven: `git apply --check` clean against HEAD; the patched real loader reproduces the Crypto census exactly (14,839 rows loaded, 80 refused, identical sub_id set); a live console run whose database has the columns still returns real street values (250 rows, street_1 non-blank 250). Not applied; Paul applies. Without it, or without the S2 lane's street backfill, no rebuild starts regardless of the rulings.
