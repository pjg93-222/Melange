  MECHANICS, stated from the quotes above:
   * the official record carries NO "superseded" field.  eligible() (line 89-91) returns False
     on EITHER  fact["withdrawn"] is True  OR  canonical_role(row role) != canonical_role(fact role).
   * build_archive_legislative.py:206-208 collapses both into the SINGLE disposition string
     "official-membership-withdrawn-or-role-superseded", so the string alone cannot tell them apart:
     the only way to separate them is to read fact["withdrawn"] out of the official record.
   * role-supersession in practice = the archive row says "cosponsor" while BILLSTATUS lists the
     person among original_cosponsors (official role "original" -> canonical "original-cosponsor").
   * build() writes withdrawn/withdrawn_date per person (line 43) and only proposes an ADDITION
     when the membership is NOT withdrawn and the role actually changed (line 50-54).
