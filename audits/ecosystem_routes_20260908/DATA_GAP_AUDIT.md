## Priority and completion criteria

1. Resolve high-value person and client/registrant identity candidates already supported by held evidence, including the targeted Quorum–Politico cases.
2. Turn adjudicated LDA covered positions and dated LittleSis employment/professional roles into explicitly typed routes. Complete Kathleen's career chronology and test similar people across different organization types.
3. Backfill pre-2021 LDA filings and refresh after the current cutoff. Track completeness by year, registrant and source ID, with amendment handling.
4. Fill the workplace team and date gaps needed for proximity × time; retain role-level internship exclusions and unknown intervals.
5. Rebuild the coverage ledger and evaluate missed as well as false connections against an independently reviewed sample. The 42-person software/source evaluation is not a ground-truth network benchmark.

For each person, measure known roles, roles with usable dates, reconciled source IDs, observed relationship types, unresolved leads, and the earliest/latest source coverage. For each organization and source-year, measure available versus expected records where an external denominator exists. A zero route count should carry its coverage reason; it must never imply that the person has no real connections.

There is currently no defensible percentage for how much of the real-world network is missing. The system has not established that denominator, and the counts above must not be summed into one missing-data total.
