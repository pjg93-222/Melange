Name the staffer roster alone in the left-out line when the comparison reads no payroll evidence

Finding D1 of the re-check. With work_sources=directory the work index holds directory episodes only, so nobody has payroll evidence there and "uncovered" means "not on the staffer roster": a person with payroll rows and no roster entry is left out of that comparison. The page said that the staff "lack the payroll or staffer-roster coverage", which suggests that payroll could have covered them.

Every office answer now carries uncovered_basis beside uncovered_staff_count and uncovered_staff_ids: payroll_or_roster, or roster when work_sources is directory (ClosenessRoutes.route; distinct_routes hands it to every route as it does the other two). It is decided by the mode, because that is what the model can use, and not by whether the world happens to hold payroll rows. The page picks the wording. For roster it reads "N of this office's current staff lack the staffer-roster coverage this model requires and were left out." and, for one person, "1 of this office's current staff lacks the staffer-roster coverage this model requires and was left out."; for payroll_or_roster, for any other value and for none (a page in front of an older server) it reads what it read before. The staffer_live README says it.
